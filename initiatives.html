Immediate initiatives

What we do

YDPA's immediate work focuses on two complementary programs: getting life-saving resources into students' hands, and building a peer network that spreads accurate information from the inside out.

Initiative 01

Resource Distribution

We distribute evidence-based harm reduction materials directly to students in Oakland Unified School District. Our kits include fentanyl test strips, naloxone (Narcan), and informational guides on recognizing overdose and seeking help.

Fentanyl test strips

Allow students to test substances for fentanyl contamination before use.

Naloxone (Narcan)

Opioid overdose reversal medication, distributed with training on how to use it.

Informational guides

Plain-language guides on drug risks, overdose recognition, and how to get help.

Initiative 02

Ambassador Program

Peer education is among the most effective tools in public health. Our Ambassador Program trains student volunteers to become trusted sources of drug safety information within their own schools and social networks.

Peer-led training

Ambassadors receive comprehensive training in harm reduction principles, overdose response, and how to have non-judgmental conversations about drug use.

School outreach

Trained ambassadors host informal sessions, distribute resources, and serve as a first point of contact for peers who need help or information.

Community building

The program creates a network of informed, empathetic students who can shift the culture around drug use from shame to safety.

Ongoing support

YDPA provides continued mentorship and updated materials to all ambassadors throughout the school year.
