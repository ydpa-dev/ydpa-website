What we do

Three programs. One mutual goal.

Each program designed and uniquely tailored to schools and the needs of their student body. Our programs are aimed to destigmatize drug safety and promote student leadership.

Program 1

Free & easy access

Drug safety resources distributed to every high school student enrolled in partner schools / districts.

  • Naloxone (nasal spray), training to accompay it
  • Drug testing strips, informational brochures about their pros and cons.
  • Brochures including where to access more safety resources, ways to get involved, and what to do in an emergency.
Program 2

The Ambassador Program

A small, student-led team at every partner school that keeps the program running and encourages leadership and advocacy.

  • 1–3 student ambassadors, 1–2 faculty members, and one administrator per school
  • Monthly meetings within each school; bimonthly meetings across the district
  • Biweekly Friday newsletters sharing educational resources, volunteer opportunities, and where to find drug safety resources
