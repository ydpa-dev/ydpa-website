About us

Who we are

The Youth Drug Protection Alliance is a youth-led organization dedicated to reducing drug-related harm among high schoolers in the Bay Area, founded in Oakland by Noah Jayne.

Our mission

Drug protection ≠ drug prevention

YDPA's purpose is to secure protections and rights for minors in terms of drug usage. We aim to distribute drug safety resources across various school districts, beginning with Oakland Unified School District. Rather than focusing on prevention, YDPA is committed to safety for youth who use drugs.

Why now?
41%

of grades 9–12 students reported using illicit substances to get “high” at least once in their lives.

California Healthy Kids Survey, 2019–2020

12%

of students reported using substances on school property in the 30 days prior to taking the survey.

California Healthy Kids Survey, 2019–2020

Realistically and unfortunately, the phenomenon of underage students engaging with illicit alcohol or drugs isn’t going to stop tomorrow. As long as underage drug usage exists, YDPA vows to work towards the users’ safety and protection.
