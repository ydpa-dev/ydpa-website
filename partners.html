Get involved

There are a few ways to work with us.

For schools

Bring the Ambassador Program to your campus

We provide the structure, drug safety resources, and training; your school provides the space and the students and staff willing to lead it. We start small with a pilot program before scaling to other schools in the district.

Start a conversation
For organizations

Partner as a harm reduction or health org

We're building relationships with organizations already doing this work — on supply, training, referrals, and shared advocacy.

Reach out
For volunteers & donors

Support the work directly

Our 501(c)(3) nonprofit status is in process. In the meantime, email us — we're looking for board members, volunteers, and early supporters who want to help shape this from the ground up.

Current status

Piloting at a partner high school in Oakland.

We're in early conversations with our first pilot school, along with Alameda County health partners and Bay Area harm reduction organizations. Details on named partners will be posted here as those relationships are formalized.
